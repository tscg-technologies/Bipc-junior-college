Master Physical Chemistry laws, Organic reaction mechanisms, Inorganic periodic trends and precision analytical laboratory techniques.

Molecular Science

The Central Molecular Science

Chemistry stands as the bridge between macroscopic physical phenomena and microscopic biological operations. For BiPC students, chemical literacy is the gateway to understanding cellular respiration, pharmaceutical drug action, and physiological buffers.

Our curriculum organizes Chemistry into three harmonized branches: Physical Chemistry (energy, equilibrium, kinetics), Organic Chemistry (carbon chains, functional group transformations, biomolecules), and Inorganic Chemistry (periodic classification, coordination complexes, bonding).

Laboratory practical sessions teach volumetric titration, qualitative salt analysis, and laboratory safety with disciplined precision.

Illustrative Curriculum

The Three Pillars of Intermediate Chemistry

Illustrative topic modules across Physical, Organic, and Inorganic Chemistry.

Branch 01

Physical Chemistry

Mathematical relationships governing chemical energetics and reaction rates.

  • • Atomic Structure: Quantum numbers, orbitals & de Broglie wavelength.
  • • Chemical Bonding: VSEPR, hybridization & molecular orbital theory.
  • • Thermodynamics: Enthalpy ($\Delta H$), entropy ($\Delta S$) & Gibbs free energy ($\Delta G$).
  • • Chemical Equilibrium: Le Chatelier's principle, $K_c$, $K_p$ & buffer solutions.
  • • Solutions: Raoult’s law, colligative properties & van 't Hoff factor.
  • • Chemical Kinetics: Reaction rate laws, half-life & Arrhenius activation energy.
Branch 02

Organic Chemistry

Carbon compounds, reaction mechanisms, synthesis, and biomolecules.

  • • Hydrocarbons: Alkanes, alkenes, alkynes & aromatic electrophilic substitution.
  • • Functional Groups: Haloalkanes, nucleophilic substitution ($S_N1$, $S_N2$).
  • • Organic Reactions: Elimination, oxidation, reduction & Markovnikov additions.
  • • Alcohols & Phenols: Acidic nature, Lucas test & Reimer-Tiemann reaction.
  • • Aldehydes & Ketones: Nucleophilic addition, aldol condensation, Cannizzaro.
  • • Biomolecules: Carbohydrates, proteins, amino acids, enzymes & nucleic acids.
Branch 03

Inorganic Chemistry

Periodic trends, electronic configurations, and coordination compounds.

  • • Periodic Classification: Electronegativity, ionization energy & atomic radii trends.
  • • Chemical Elements: Hydrogen, alkali and alkaline earth metals (s-block).
  • • Coordination Compounds: Werner’s theory, ligands, crystal field theory (CFT) & IUPAC naming.
  • • p-Block Elements: Group 13 to 18 trends, oxoacids of nitrogen, phosphorus, sulfur & halogens.
  • • d-Block & f-Block Elements: Transition metals, oxidation states, lanthanoid contraction & color.
Syllabus Notice: These topic modules are illustrative for Intermediate Board programs. Exact chapter titles and weightages are guided by current official Board circulars.
Laboratory Skills

Practical Chemistry & Laboratory Safety Awareness

The Chemistry laboratory develops precision in volumetric titrations, qualitative salt analysis, and safe reagent handling.

  • • Volumetric Titrations: Standardizing $KMnO_4$ against oxalic acid and Mohr’s salt solutions.
  • • Qualitative Salt Analysis: Systematic identification of cations ($Pb^{2+}$, $Cu^{2+}$, $Fe^{3+}$, $Al^{3+}$, $Zn^{2+}$) and anions ($CO_3^{2-}$, $SO_4^{2-}$, $Cl^-$).
  • • Organic Functional Group Tests: Unsaturation testing with bromine water, Tollens' reagent, and neutral $FeCl_3$.
  • • Rigorous Safety Protocols: Mandatory lab coats, safety goggles, chemical fume exhaust hoods, and acid-spill neutralizers.
