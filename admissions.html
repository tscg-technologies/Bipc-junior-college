Admissions 2026–2027

Enrolling in the BiPC Intermediate Program

Admission to the two-year Intermediate BiPC stream is open to students who have successfully completed Class 10 (SSC, CBSE, ICSE, or equivalent recognized state/national examinations).

Candidates should demonstrate a strong interest in biological science, basic scientific curiosity, and a willingness to engage consistently in laboratory practical work and disciplined self-study.

Official Board Notice: Eligibility criteria, subject minimums, and admission procedures are subject to the applicable State Intermediate Education Board regulations and the institution's verified admission policy.

Documents Required for Admission

Please keep clear original copies and photocopies ready during the official admission verification:

  • Class 10 Marksheet / Passing Certificate:

    Original and attested copies verifying Class 10 board completion.

  • Transfer Certificate (TC):

    Issued by the previous school / secondary institution where applicable.

  • Identity & Address Proof:

    Aadhaar card or recognized government identification of student and parent.

  • Recent Passport-Size Photographs:

    Standard color passport photographs (minimum 4 copies).

  • Other Documents:

    Caste / income certificate / migration certificate where applicable as required by board rules.

Note on Cutoffs & Fees: Admission cutoffs, fees, and seat availability are determined strictly by official institution circulars and governing board policies. No unofficial estimates or guarantees apply.
